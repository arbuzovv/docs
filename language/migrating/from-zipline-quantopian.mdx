---
title: "From Zipline & Quantopian"
description: "The closest possible migration path"
---

If your strategy runs on **Zipline** or **zipline-reloaded**, or was written for **Quantopian**, you are migrating to the engine Ziplime grew out of. The programming model is the same — `initialize`, `handle_data`, `context`, `data`, `order_target_percent`, `schedule_function`, pipelines.

Three things change in the code:

- Everything that touches the engine is **awaited**, and the hooks are `async def`.
- `data.current` and `data.history` take lists and return **Polars** frames, so `.iloc` and `.loc` become `bars["close"][-1]`.
- Orders take an **execution style**: `style=MarketOrder()`.

And one thing changes in the results: fills happen on the **next bar** by default in the CLI and the MCP server, and trading controls are enforced. A migrated backtest usually reports less than the original — that gap is the look-ahead the original had.

The step-by-step guide, with a conversion table and a full before/after example, is [Migrating from Classic Zipline](/language/strategy-language/zipline-migration).

<Note>
  Quantopian research often uses APIs that never made it into open-source Zipline (`quantopian.pipeline.data`, Morningstar fundamentals, `Q500US` filters). In Ziplime, named universes come from `context.universe_symbols(name)` when your asset database carries them and fundamentals from [point-in-time datasets](/language/strategy-language/point-in-time-datasets) or your own data sources.
</Note>
